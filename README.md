MoneyMate

I built MoneyMate to solve my own day-to-day expense management problem. Most finance apps felt too complicated, too lengthy, or packed with features I didn't need. I wanted something simple — buy something, record the expense right away, and get on with my day. No hassle, no unnecessary features, just money management made easy.
